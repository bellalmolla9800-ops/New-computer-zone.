QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Have a question that isn't covered here? Contact us and we'll help clarify what you need.

Ask us a question ↗
What should I bring for an online application?+

Requirements vary by application. Bring the relevant ID or certificates, a working mobile number or email if required, and any photo or signature files requested by the official portal. Ask us before visiting if you're unsure.

Can you guarantee my application will be approved?+

No. We can assist with the process, but decisions, eligibility and processing times are determined by the relevant authority or service provider.

Do you offer colour printing and scanning?+

Printing, scanning and photocopy options may vary by location and equipment. Contact us to confirm availability and current charges.

How much do services cost?+

Charges depend on the service, page count, colour, portal fees and other applicable costs. We recommend confirming the total before the work begins.

How do you handle my personal documents?+

Share only the information required for your task. Review documents before submission, avoid sharing passwords or OTPs unnecessarily, and ask us to delete temporary copies when they are no longer needed.
