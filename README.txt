INTER CYBER CAFE & ONLINE SERVICES — WEBSITE PACKAGE

Files:
- index.html: Website content and page structure
- style.css: Responsive design for desktop, tablet and mobile
- script.js: Mobile navigation, enquiry form and WhatsApp integration

BEFORE PUBLISHING:
1. Open index.html in a browser to preview.
2. In index.html, replace the sample address, phone, email, hours and business details with your real information.
3. In script.js, replace WHATSAPP_NUMBER = '91XXXXXXXXXX' with your WhatsApp number, including country code, digits only (example format: 919876543210).
4. Review the services and remove any service you do not offer or are not authorized to provide.
5. Upload all three website files to your hosting provider. Keep the file names and their relative locations unchanged.

The contact form opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message; it does not store submissions on a server.
The site uses Google Fonts from the internet; fallback fonts are included.
